There is a particular kind of learning that only happens when you refuse the
starter template. Not because templates are bad, but because they answer
questions you never got round to asking.

So: an empty directory, and a rule that nothing goes in until I can say why.

## The first real decision

Content types before components. It's tempting to start with the visual layer,
because that's the part you can see — but the data model is the thing that's
expensive to change later, and the stylesheet is the thing that's cheap.

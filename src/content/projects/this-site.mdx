The previous version of this site was a template with my name in it. It worked,
it looked reasonable, and I could not have told you why any single decision in
it had been made.

## The rule

An empty directory, and nothing added until I could say what it was for. That
turned out to be a much slower way to build a website and a much faster way to
understand one.

The content model came before the components, which felt backwards for about a
week and then stopped feeling backwards at all. Collections are shaped like the
things they store; a thin view layer handles drafts, sorting, and the combined
feeds. Pages never touch the content API directly, which means the draft filter
exists in exactly one place and cannot be forgotten on the next page I add.

## Still in progress

Notes, the stash, and the actual visual design. The current stylesheet is empty
on purpose — it is easier to tell whether the markup is right when nothing is
hiding it.
